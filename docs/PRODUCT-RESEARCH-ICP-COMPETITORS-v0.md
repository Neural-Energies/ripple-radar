## ICP outline (hypotheses — to kill or keep in interviews)

### Primary (first conversations)

Event-driven / global macro PMs and senior analysts at multi-strategy and macro funds who:

- Already pay for a Terminal
- Already live in news / world tape
- Still jump from headline → obvious ticker by hand
- Care about second- and third-order exposures on a multi-day horizon

**Job to be done (hypothesis):**  
“A shock just hit. I need the second-order book in minutes: who moves, through what bottleneck, what would invalidate, what is crowded vs confirmed.”

**Economic buyer (hypothesis):** PM / head of macro / CIO-lite — not IR, not retail growth.

### Secondary (later, not day-one)

- Prop / family-office macro desks with the same JTBD but thinner research stack
- Macro-adjacent commodity / shipping / energy specialists who already think in bottlenecks

### Anti-ICP (hard filters — Sales should disqualify early)

- Anyone who wants `BUY X 87%` or calibrated probs as day-one table stakes
- Anyone shopping for a cheaper Terminal or a news/alerts app
- Millisecond / same-session news-arb desks (wrong edge thesis)
- Crypto-native shops that need funding/basis as confirmation (we do not have it yet)
- Buyers who will not accept “research prototype” framing under Phase 0–1

### Interview plan (outline — no outreach until Joshua unlocks)

**Goal:** Validate / kill ICP, JTBD, anti-ICP, and WTP packaging hypotheses — not sell.

**N (proposed):** 8–12 structured discovery interviews once Phase 1 is unlocked; founder-led for first 3–5 design-partner tracks.

**Core questions (map to Sales discovery opener):**

1. Shock → book: where does second-order work live today? Who owns the causal map?
2. How do they pick non-obvious exposures vs the obvious ticker?
3. How do they invalidate mid-event? What breaks the thesis?
4. Horizon: days+ structural vs same-session? (same-session → poor fit)
5. Do they require calibrated probs before a tool sits on the desk? (yes → not day-one)
6. Current stack: Terminal + news + notes — where is the gap they feel?
7. Budget / packaging: desk package vs seat? Who signs? Kill-criteria for a pilot?
8. What would make a research-prototype pilot *fail* in their eyes?

**Success criteria for Research (not CS pilot criteria):** written kill/keep on each ICP hypothesis; competitor feature map with sellability notes; WTP bands labeled as hypotheses only.

**Out of scope for interviews:** inventing NPS/% preference; quoting fake “X% of PMs said…”; any public case study while Phase 0 holds.

## Competitor teardown outline

Three buckets only. Method: public product pages + honest claim inventory + “what research object they assemble.” No invented market shares or win rates.

### A. Terminal / institutional data workstations

| Lens | What to capture |
|---|---|
| Examples | Bloomberg Terminal, Refinitiv / LSEG Workspace, FactSet (as kitchen-sink workstations) |
| They sell | Data, news, functions, execution adjacency, the place to work |
| Gap we claim | They do not assemble event → causal graph → ranked 2nd/3rd-order exposures as a first-class research object |
| Honesty check | We are **not** a Terminal replacement; we sit beside it |
| Demo implication | Never pitch “cheaper Bloomberg”; pitch the research object the Terminal does not build |

**Teardown checklist:** news → analytics path; any causal / scenario native UI; exposure ranking logic; probability presentation (costume % risk); pricing posture (seat vs desk — public only).

### B. News apps / briefing / alert products

| Lens | What to capture |
|---|---|
| Examples | Reuters / Bloomberg news surfaces, briefing newsletters, X/alert stacks, “AI news → ticker” tools |
| They sell | Speed to headline / summary |
| Gap we claim | They stop at the headline; we refuse headline → ticker |
| Honesty check | Speed alone is not our wedge; structural / multi-day is |
| Demo implication | Show Map before tickers; never race headlines |

**Teardown checklist:** latency positioning; ticker deep-links; any second-order language; signal/probability claims (flag as competitor banned-for-us if they costume %).

### C. Macro research shops / expert networks / note mills

| Lens | What to capture |
|---|---|
| Examples | Sell-side / independent macro notes, expert networks, thematic research boutiques |
| They sell | Opinion, once (PDF / call) |
| Gap we claim | Event-agnostic living book that rebuilds as evidence arrives — not a canned-event dashboard or a static note |
| Honesty check | We do not ship “our house view replaces your analyst” |
| Demo implication | Living book + invalidation > authored narrative

**Teardown checklist:** update cadence; whether graph/transmission is explicit; how they handle unknown shocks; packaging (subscription note vs tool).

### Cross-cut questions (all three buckets)

1. Do they force graph-before-tickers or headline-before-tickers?
2. Importance vs probability — do they conflate?
3. Event-agnostic vs canned themes?
4. Edge thesis: ms / same-session vs structural multi-day?
5. What can Sales honestly say we do that they don’t — without overclaim?
