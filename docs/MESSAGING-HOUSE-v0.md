## One-line category

Institutional event-driven macro workstation — world tape → event book → causal graph → ranked second- and third-order exposures.

Not a news app. Not a cheaper Terminal. Not a buy/sell signal.

---

## Three pillars

1. **Graph before tickers**  
   Shock hits → build the causal map first. Tickers are leaves, not the entry point. Refuse headline → ticker.

2. **Importance ≠ probability**  
   Rank what matters for the book by causal distance and transmission — not by a costume %. Scenarios are family-appropriate; numbers only when freeze-at-T and provenance say so.

3. **Event-agnostic living book**  
   No canned-event dashboard. Unknown shocks still construct a full book as evidence arrives. Target edge is structural / multi-day, not millisecond arb.

## Anti-ICP → banned phrases (from Product Research)

Never pitch language that attracts or flatters these buyers:

| Anti-ICP | Banned / avoid phrases |
|---|---|
| Wants `BUY X 87%` | “signal,” “edge score,” “model confidence,” costume %, win-rate screenshots |
| Shopping cheaper Terminal / news app | “Bloomberg alternative,” “Terminal killer,” “all-in-one news,” “faster alerts” |
| ms / same-session arb | “real-time edge,” “same-session confirmation,” “beat the tape” |
| Crypto funding/basis day-one | “crypto first-class,” basis/funding as confirmation |
| Won’t accept research-prototype framing | “production-ready,” “live calibrated,” case studies / logos |
